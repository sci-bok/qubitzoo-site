Demonstrates fast initialization, control, and readout of a 14 MHz heavy-fluxonium qubit using higher levels and nonadiabatic flux pulses rather than ordinary resonant microwave control.

## Key Results
- Heavy-fluxonium transition frequency: 14 MHz
- Initialization fidelity: 97%, corresponding to an effective temperature of $190\,\mu\mathrm{K}$
- Fast-flux single-qubit gates: 20–60 ns
- Average single-qubit gate fidelity: 99.8%
- Coherence: $T_1,T_{2e}\sim300\,\mu\mathrm{s}$
