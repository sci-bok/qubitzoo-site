Demonstrates high-fidelity microwave control of a naturally strained SnV⁻ center while retaining stable optical transitions. Author order was verified against the PDF title page.

## Key Results
- Microwave $\pi$-pulse fidelity: $99.51(3)\%$
- Hahn-echo coherence: $T_2=170.0(2.8)\,\mu\text{s}$
- XY16 coherence: $650(28)\,\mu\text{s}$
- Operating temperature: 1.7 K
