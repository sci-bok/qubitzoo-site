Two-dimensional cesium-array milestone for Rydberg-mediated entangling gates in optical tweezers.

## Key Results
- Implemented CZ gates between selected pairs inside a 121-site $^{133}\mathrm{Cs}$ tweezer array.
- Reported Bell-state fidelity 0.86(2), improving to 0.89 after SPAM correction.
- Reported CZ gate fidelity 0.91 including one-qubit errors.
- Made clear that motion and laser phase noise, rather than missing blockade physics, dominated the error budget at that stage.
