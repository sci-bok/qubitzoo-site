This paper introduced the capacitively shunted phase-qubit design, separating the shunt capacitor from the tunnel junction to reduce strongly coupled two-level defects.

## Key Results
- Reduced the density of coupled two-level systems by roughly an order of magnitude
- Improved destructive single-shot measurement fidelity to about 90%
- Demonstrated single-shot quantum state tomography
