Systematic study of gatemon frequency stability, hysteresis, dephasing, and shunt-capacitor design.

## Key Results

- Reliable qubit-frequency placement with 1 MHz precision across a range of several gigahertz.
- Grounded shunt-capacitor gatemons were more stable than floating-capacitor designs.
- Stability and dephasing remained strongly frequency- and device-design-dependent.
