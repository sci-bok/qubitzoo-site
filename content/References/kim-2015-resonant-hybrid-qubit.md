Introduces resonant microwave detuning control at a charge-noise-protected operating point of the quantum-dot hybrid qubit.

## Key Results
- Reports $X$-axis $\pi$ rotations in under 5 ns with fidelity above 93%.
- Reports $Z$ rotations in 50 ps with fidelity above 96%.
- Extends coherence beyond 150 ns with Hahn echo and Carr-Purcell sequences.
