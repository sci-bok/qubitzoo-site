Demonstrates time-bin photonic-qubit storage in $^{151}\mathrm{Eu}^{3+}$:Y₂SiO₅ with a $27.6 \pm 0.5\,\mathrm{s}$ 1/e lifetime, $88.0 \pm 2.1\%$ fidelity at 5.6 s, and single-photon-level storage out to 42 s.
