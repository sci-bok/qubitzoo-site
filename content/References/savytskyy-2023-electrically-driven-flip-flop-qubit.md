First experimental demonstration of coherent microwave-electric control of a single $^{31}\text{P}$ electron-nuclear flip-flop qubit in silicon.

## Key Results

- Demonstrated a $118.5(2.5)\,\text{kHz}$ flip-flop Rabi frequency through hyperfine Stark modulation.
- Measured an average native one-qubit gate fidelity of $98.4(2)\%$ by randomized benchmarking.
- Measured $T_1^{\mathrm{ff}}=173(12)\,\text{s}$, Ramsey $T_2^*=4.09(88)\,\mu\text{s}$, and Hahn-echo $T_2^{\mathrm H}=184(24)\,\mu\text{s}$.
- Did not reach the donor-interface large-dipole regime or demonstrate a two-qubit gate.
