Hardware-efficient mid-circuit erasure check for a dual-rail qubit in two superconducting cavities. A strong parametric beamsplitter makes the ancilla spectrum depend on joint photon number, allowing total-excitation information to be measured without resolving the logical rail.

## Key Results
- Missed-erasure fraction: $(9.0\pm0.5)\times10^{-4}$
- Erasure rate per check: $2.92\pm0.01\%$
- Pauli error rate per check: $0.31\pm0.01\%$
