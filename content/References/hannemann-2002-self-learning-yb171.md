An early experimental use of the two ground-state hyperfine levels of a single trapped $^{171}$Yb$^+$ ion as a qubit. The title-page author order was verified against the published PDF.

## Key Results
- Coherent microwave manipulation of a single $^{171}$Yb$^+$ hyperfine qubit
- Adaptive quantum-state estimation using sequential measurements
- Establishes a demonstrated $^{171}$Yb$^+$ hyperfine qubit by 2002, five years before the later comprehensive characterization
