This paper demonstrates telecom-wavelength time-bin entanglement from a hybrid photonic integrated circuit, showing that integrated time-bin hardware can reach network-relevant entanglement quality rather than remaining a purely bulk-optics encoding.

## Key Results
- Demonstrates telecom-wavelength time-bin entangled photon pairs from a hybrid photonic integrated circuit
- Reports Bell-state fidelity of $96^{+2}_{-5}\%$
- Reports concurrence of $96^{+3}_{-8}\%$
- Strengthens the case for compact, fiber-pigtailed, mass-deployable time-bin networking hardware
