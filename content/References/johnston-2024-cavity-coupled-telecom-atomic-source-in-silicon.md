This paper reports a cavity-coupled single-T-centre source in silicon, pushing the platform beyond passive integration toward a genuinely stronger spin-photon interface for networking.

## Key Results
- Single T centres coupled to low-loss silicon photonic crystal cavities
- Purcell-enhanced fluorescence decay-rate increase of **F = 6.89**
- Average zero-phonon-line photon outcoupling rate of **73.3 kHz** under saturation
- System detection efficiency of **9.1%**, about 20x better than a typical confocal-style setup
