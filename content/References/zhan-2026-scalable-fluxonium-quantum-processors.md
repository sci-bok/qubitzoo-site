Demonstrates a modular fluxonium-transmon-fluxonium tunable-coupler architecture at processor scale.

## Key Results

- Parallel single-qubit fidelities approaching $99.99\%$.
- Best CZ fidelity $99.9\%$ at 32 ns, with two-qubit fidelities around $99\%$ across the system.
- A 22-qubit processor generated GHZ states involving up to 10 qubits.
