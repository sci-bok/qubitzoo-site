Demonstrates high-coherence fluxonium devices in a fabrication flow designed for wafer-scale yield and junction uniformity.

## Key Results

- Nearly 100% junction yield across a 2-inch wafer.
- Less than 5% variation for the phase-slip junction and less than 2% variation for the full junction array.
- Energy-relaxation times exceeding $1\,\text{ms}$ at the flux-frustration point.
