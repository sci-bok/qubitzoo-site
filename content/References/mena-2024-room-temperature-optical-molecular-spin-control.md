Mena et al. demonstrated optically detected coherent control of photoexcited pentacene triplet spins at room temperature in a molecular crystal and a thermally evaporated film.

## Key Results
- Observed room-temperature Rabi oscillations with photoluminescence contrast exceeding $15\%$.
- Retained microsecond-scale coherence in both bulk-crystal and thin-film realizations.
- Demonstrated a molecular spin–optical interface aimed primarily at quantum sensing and imaging.
