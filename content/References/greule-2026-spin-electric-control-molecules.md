Greule et al. demonstrated highly localized exchange-mediated spin-electric tuning and all-electrical coherent control of individual FePc and coupled Fe–FePc molecular spins using ESR-STM.

## Key Results
- Observed nonlinear resonance shifts approaching $30\%$ near the FePc LUMO.
- Demonstrated voltage-controlled detuning of Rabi oscillations in single and coupled molecular-spin systems.
- Identified transport-mediated exchange bias from the magnetic STM tip as the dominant tuning mechanism.
