Established $^{171}$Yb nuclear-spin qubits in optical tweezers as a serious coherence-first neutral-atom platform, combining high loading efficiency, fast control, and multi-second coherence.

## Key Results
- Loaded a 10$\times$10 tweezer array with 92.73(8)% efficiency and a single tweezer with 96.0(1.4)% efficiency.
- Measured sub-microsecond single-qubit control with 5.2(5)$\times 10^{-3}$ error per Clifford gate.
- Reported $T_2^* = 3.7(4)$ s and spin-echo $T_2 = 7.9(4)$ s.
- Found spin depolarization times of tens of seconds, extendable toward the 100 s scale with a several-gauss magnetic bias field.
