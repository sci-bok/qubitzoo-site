Resonantly driven and continuously dressed $S$–$T_0$ hole-spin qubit in a germanium double quantum dot operated at low magnetic field and the detuning symmetry point.

## Key Results
- 99.68(2)% average physical-gate fidelity with $T_2^*=1.9\,\mu$s for the bare resonantly driven qubit.
- Dressing increased rotating-frame coherence to $T_{2\rho}^*=20.3\,\mu$s while retaining 99.63(7)% gate fidelity.
