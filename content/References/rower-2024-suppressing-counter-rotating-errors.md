Demonstrates few-cycle, high-fidelity fluxonium control using circularly polarized and commensurate pulses to suppress counter-rotating errors beyond the rotating-wave approximation.

## Key Results
- Best measured average single-qubit gate fidelity: $99.99807(7)\%$.
- Optimized commensurate $X$ pulse duration: approximately $8.2\,\mathrm{ns}$.
- The best gates were coherence limited and remained stable over 34 hours after calibration.
