## Citation
T. Tanttu et al., "Assessment of the errors of high-fidelity two-qubit gates in silicon quantum dots," [Nature Physics **20**, 1804–1809 (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-024-02614-w) · [arXiv:2303.04090](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.04090).

## Key Results
- Demonstrated repeatable above-99% two-qubit operation in multiple SiMOS devices
- Reported an average interleaved-randomized-benchmarking fidelity of 99.17% across three devices
- Traced important errors to slow nuclear and electrical noise plus sequence-dependent contextual noise
