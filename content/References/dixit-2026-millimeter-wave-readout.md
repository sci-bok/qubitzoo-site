Extends circuit-QED readout into the millimeter-wave band by coupling a conventional transmon to a highly detuned 34.7-GHz three-dimensional cavity mode.

## Key Results
- 34.7-GHz cavity coupled to a 3.1-GHz transmon at $g/2\pi=1.3$ GHz.
- No resonant state transitions observed up to 1,000 drive photons.
- Greater than 99% readout fidelity with more than 100 photons and no quantum-limited first-stage amplifier.
