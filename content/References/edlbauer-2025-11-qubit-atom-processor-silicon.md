This paper reports an 11-qubit phosphorus-in-silicon atom processor composed of two multi-nuclear-spin donor registers linked by electron exchange interaction.

## Key Results
- 11-qubit donor processor with 9 nuclear-spin qubits and 2 electron-spin ancilla/control qubits
- High-fidelity single- and multi-qubit operations across local and non-local donor-register pairs
- Bell-state fidelities up to 99.5% and GHZ-state generation across eight nuclear spins
- Strong evidence that interconnected donor-spin registers can scale beyond isolated few-qubit Kane-style devices
