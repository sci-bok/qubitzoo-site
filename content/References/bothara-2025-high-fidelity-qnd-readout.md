Characterizes single-shot dispersive readout and measurement back-action in a tantalum-based fluxonium device.

## Key Results

- Published assignment fidelity $96.2\pm0.5\%$ without a parametric amplifier and $97.8\pm0.4\%$ with one.
- The amplified result used a 260 ns integration time.
- Published QND repeatability $99.0\pm0.3\%$; state-mixing errors and measurement-induced transitions were the main limitations.
