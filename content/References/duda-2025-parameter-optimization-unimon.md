Derives accurate approximations for single-well inductively shunted qubits and identifies a theoretical unimon design regime near $E_J=E_L$, 1--2 k$\Omega$ mode impedance, and 1 GHz qubit frequency with predicted single-qubit fidelity above 99.99%.
