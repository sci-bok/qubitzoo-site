This paper demonstrates quantum teleportation over 100 km of telecom fiber using highly efficient superconducting nanowire single-photon detectors, cementing time-bin encoding as a practical long-distance networking qubit.

## Key Results
- Demonstrates quantum teleportation over 100 km of telecom fiber
- Uses superconducting nanowire single-photon detectors to keep the long-distance Bell measurement viable
- Serves as a canonical milestone for time-bin photonic networking beyond laboratory-scale links
