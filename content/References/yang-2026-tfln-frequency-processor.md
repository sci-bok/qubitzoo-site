## Abstract

Demonstrates a monolithic thin-film-lithium-niobate processor for coherent and programmable control of frequency-encoded photonic qubits using microwave-driven coupled resonators.

## Key Results

- Reported arbitrary single-qubit rotations with an average control metric of $97.1(6)\%$.
- Demonstrated an ancilla-free frequency-bin controlled-phase gate with lower-bound process fidelity $91.4(14)\%$.
- Measured Hong-Ou-Mandel interference visibility of $94.9(18)\%$ without background subtraction.
