## Citation
Members of the HRL Quantum Team and Collaborators, "A digitally controlled silicon quantum processing unit," [Nature **655**, 1154–1159 (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10754-7) · [arXiv:2604.16216](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16216).

## Key Results
- Integrated a 4 K cryogenic-CMOS controller, superconducting ribbon interconnect, and a 54-dot Si/SiGe quantum chip configurable for up to 18 three-spin exchange-only qubits
- Reported mean errors of $1.7\times10^{-4}$ for 1Q gates and $3.5\times10^{-3}$ for CNOT, with a lowest reproducible CNOT error of $9\times10^{-4}$
- Demonstrated a distance-5 repetition code and a six-physical-qubit $[[4,2,2]]$ quantum error-detecting code
