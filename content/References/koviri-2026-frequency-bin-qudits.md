## Abstract

Synthesizes discrete, high-dimensional, frequency-entangled photonic states from a broadband biphoton state using time-domain Fourier optics, with bin spacings compatible with telecommunications grids.

## Key Results

- Observed spectral anticorrelations across 38 frequency bins with spacings from 12.5 to 750 GHz.
- Certified a lower-bound frequency-binned Hilbert-space dimension of 289, corresponding to two 17-dimensional qudits.
- Demonstrated frequency-correlation nonlocality over a campus-scale two-node fibre link.
