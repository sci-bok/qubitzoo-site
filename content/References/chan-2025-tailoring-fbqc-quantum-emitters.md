Adapts fusion-based photonic quantum computing to deterministic quantum-emitter hardware, showing that the FBQC resource-state and decoder choices can be co-designed with emitter-specific noise channels rather than treated as an abstract photonic platform.

## Key Results
- Proposes FBQC resource states and fusion schedules tailored to quantum-emitter source capabilities.
- Reports thresholds of about 8% photon loss and 4% emitter-to-emitter distinguishability mismatch in the modeled architecture.
- Finds spin-noise tolerances comfortably above typical memory-induced errors for relevant spin-photon interfaces.
