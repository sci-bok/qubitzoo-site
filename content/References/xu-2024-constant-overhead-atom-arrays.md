Develops a fault-tolerant qLDPC architecture that uses atom rearrangement to implement non-local product-code syndrome extraction.

## Key Results
- Simulated crossover below surface-code overhead at several hundred physical qubits for physical error rate $10^{-3}$
- Product structure enables hardware-efficient scheduling of non-local checks
- Architecture proposal; not an experimental qLDPC demonstration
