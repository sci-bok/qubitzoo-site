Demonstrates a Kerr-cat qubit in a scalable planar superconducting circuit with an on-chip band-block filter.

## Key Results
- 99.6% QND readout fidelity at a cat size of eight photons
- Coherent-state lifetime near 0.95 ms at a cat size of eight
- Universal-control primitives including an X(pi/2) gate driven by stabilization-phase modulation
