This paper reports strong coupling between a Si/SiGe resonant-exchange qubit and a high-impedance microwave resonator, establishing a modern silicon RX milestone and quantifying the role of charge noise in the cavity-coupled device.

## Key Results
- Strong coupling between a Si/SiGe RX qubit and a high-impedance microwave resonator
- Spin-photon coupling rate $g/2\pi = 65.8$ MHz
- Qubit decoherence rate $\gamma/2\pi = 21.4$ MHz
- Detuning-dependent analysis identifies charge noise as the dominant decoherence source
