Demonstration of a transmon-addressable 3D microwave cavity quantum memory with millisecond-scale single-photon relaxation and coherence.

## Key Results
- Single-photon lifetime $T_1=1.22\pm0.06$ ms
- Fock-superposition coherence $T_2=0.72\pm0.03$ ms
- MHz-rate control, encoding, and readout through a coupled transmon
