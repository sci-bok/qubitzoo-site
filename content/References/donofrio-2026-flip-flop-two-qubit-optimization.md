Spin-orbital simulation and control-optimization study for electrically driven two-qubit gates in flip-flop arrays.

## Key Results

- Used Makhlin invariants to identify operations locally equivalent to $\sqrt{i\mathrm{SWAP}}$ and $i\mathrm{SWAP}$.
- Predicted isolated-pair composite-gate infidelities of $4.72\times10^{-4}$ and $6.14\times10^{-4}$ after local $R_z$ phase compensation.
- Found that idle spectators distort the nonlocal interaction and require geometry-aware recalibration.
