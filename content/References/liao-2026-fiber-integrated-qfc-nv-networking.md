Telecom-band frequency conversion interface for NV-center photons aimed at long-distance quantum networking.

## Key Results
- Down-converted 637.2 nm photons to 1588.3 nm in a fiber-integrated PPLN-waveguide system
- Achieved approximately 9% total conversion efficiency with pump-induced noise suppressed to 154 Hz
- Predicted spin-photon entanglement fidelity above 52% after 100 km of fiber at NV-center emission rates
