## Abstract

A multi-junction circuit Fourier-engineers a cos(2φ)-dominated potential and reproduces the predicted parity-protected spectrum, while exposing flux noise from the residual first harmonic as the dominant lifetime limitation.

## Key Results

- Experimentally suppressed odd harmonics in a multi-junction superconducting circuit.
- Found good agreement between the measured transition spectrum and the effective cos(2φ) model.
- Identified $1/f$ flux noise near the symmetry point as the limiting mechanism and showed that an otherwise similar fluxonium is less vulnerable.
