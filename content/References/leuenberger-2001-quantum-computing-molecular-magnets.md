Leuenberger and Loss proposed using the spin eigenstates of molecular magnets as quantum-information states and worked out a Grover-search implementation for Fe$_8$ and Mn$_{12}$. This is the earliest landmark proposal used for the Qubit Zoo molecular-spin lineage.

## Key Results
- Identified molecular-magnet spin manifolds as addressable quantum-information states.
- Proposed pulsed-ESR control and readout for a molecular-magnet implementation of Grover search.
