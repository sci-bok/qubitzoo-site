First standalone bosonic quantum-error-correction demonstration in a fully planar superconducting circuit, using a heavy-fluxonium ancilla to control and stabilize finite-energy GKP states in an on-chip Archimedean spiral resonator.

## Key Results
- Heavy-fluxonium bit-flip lifetime: $451\pm70~\mu\mathrm{s}$
- Measurement-free GKP error correction with rapid fluxonium reset
- Pauli-averaged logical-lifetime enhancement: $1.59\pm0.05$
- First resonator control with a weakly coupled fluxonium and first standalone bosonic QEC in a fully planar superconducting architecture
