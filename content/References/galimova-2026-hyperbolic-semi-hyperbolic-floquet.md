Introduces hyperbolic and semi-hyperbolic Floquet-code families tailored to pair-measurement settings, with simulated thresholds reported under multiple photonic-oriented noise models.

## Key Results
- New {10,3} and {12,3} hyperbolic Floquet families via Wythoff/LINS construction
- Simulated thresholds around ~1.5% (EM3) and ~1.0–1.2% (SDEM3)
- Heralded-loss thresholds ~8.5–9% for {8,3} family in the reported setup
