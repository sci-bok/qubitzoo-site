## Citation
J. Y. Huang et al., "High-fidelity spin qubit operation and algorithmic initialization above 1 K," [Nature **627**, 772–777 (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07160-2).

## Key Results
- Demonstrated a silicon two-qubit processor above $1\,\mathrm{K}$
- Reported up to 99.85% single-qubit Clifford fidelity and 98.92% two-qubit fidelity
- Used algorithmic initialization and radio-frequency readout with fidelity up to 99.34%
