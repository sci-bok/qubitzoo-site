Analyzes how different linear-optical Bell-state-measurement circuits behave as fusion gadgets inside fusion-based quantum computing, translating abstract FBQC thresholds into concrete loss budgets for sources, interferometers, ancillae, and detectors.

## Key Results
- Compares several linear-optical Bell-state-measurement circuits for use as Type-II fusion devices in FBQC.
- Quantifies component-level optical-loss requirements needed to stay within the fault-tolerant regime.
- Clarifies how detector, circuit, and ancilla losses propagate into effective fusion-network errors.
