Universal control of four neighboring $S$–$T_-$ hole-spin qubits in a $2\times4$ germanium quantum-dot ladder. This is a polarized-triplet variant of the canonical $S$–$T_0$ encoding.

## Key Results
- Average single-qubit gate fidelities of 99.49(8)–99.84(1)%.
- Bell-state fidelities of 73(1)–90(1)% between neighboring qubit pairs.
- Remote Bell-state fidelity of 75(2)% across the array.
