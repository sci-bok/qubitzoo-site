Large-scale tweezer-array architecture milestone surpassing one thousand loaded neutral-atom qubits.

## Key Results
- Implemented combined two-dimensional configurations of 3000 tweezer sites with a mean of 1167(46) trapped single atoms.
- Used tiled microlens-generated arrays with independent laser sources to ease laser-power bottlenecks.
- Demonstrated efficient atom transfer between array regions, supporting zoned large-scale architectures.
- Important as a scale result, even though it is not itself a gate-fidelity benchmark.
