Demonstrates a fluxonium variant operated at the integer-flux sweet spot (zero applied magnetic field), moving the computational transition into the conventional transmon frequency range while retaining partial protection.

## Key Results

- Qubit frequency about 4 GHz with energy-relaxation quality factor $Q\approx0.7\times10^7$.
- Ramsey coherence above $100\,\mu\text{s}$.
- Average Clifford fidelity above $99.9\%$.
