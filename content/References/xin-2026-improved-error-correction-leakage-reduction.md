This work integrates leakage removal directly into transmon measurement and evaluates its effect on error-corrected memories without post-selection.

## Abstract

A DDROP-inspired leakage-reduction unit runs concurrently with dispersive measurement, removing 98.4% of leakage without adding cycle time while preserving 99.2% computational-state assignment fidelity. Three-level readout supplies leakage information to the decoder, and the combined protocol suppresses logical errors in memory and stability experiments.

## Key Findings

- 98.4% leakage-removal fraction with no measurement-time overhead.
- 99.2% computational-state assignment fidelity retained.
- Improved logical error correction without post-selection.
