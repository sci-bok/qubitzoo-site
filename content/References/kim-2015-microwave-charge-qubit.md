Demonstrates universal resonant control of a silicon semiconductor charge qubit while remaining near the zero-detuning sweet spot.

## Key Results
- Reached Rabi frequencies up to $2\,\text{GHz}$ for a $4.5\,\text{GHz}$ charge qubit.
- Implemented rotations about arbitrary Bloch-sphere axes with resonant AC control.
- Measured process fidelities above 86% using process tomography and gate-set tomography.
