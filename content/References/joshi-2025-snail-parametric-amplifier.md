Demonstrates a compact SNAIL parametric amplifier with an on-chip pump filter, flat 20 dB gain across up to 250 MHz, and simultaneous high-fidelity readout of two transmon qubits.

## Key Results
- 20 dB gain with less than 1 dB ripple across up to 250 MHz
- Intermodulation products suppressed by more than 23 dB at the compression point
- Two-qubit assignment fidelities of 99.51% and 98.55%
