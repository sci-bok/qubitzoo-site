Detailed coherence and noise study of a tunable capacitively shunted flux qubit intended for coherent quantum annealing applications.

## Key Results
- Characterizes a tunable CSFQ across a reported gap range of about $\Delta/2\pi \approx 1$ to $6.2\,\mathrm{GHz}$.
- Finds Ramsey pure-dephasing times at the symmetry point of roughly $100$ to $200\,\mathrm{ns}$ across the measured bias range, with spin-echo dephasing about $5\times$ longer.
- Identifies intrinsic low-frequency flux noise plus bias-line thermal noise as the dominant decoherence channels in this annealing-oriented operating regime.
