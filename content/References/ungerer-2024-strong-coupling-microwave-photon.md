Single-photon strong-coupling experiment using an InAs nanowire double quantum dot and a magnetic-field-resilient superconducting resonator.

## Key Results
- Spin-photon coupling $g/2\pi=139\pm4$ MHz.
- Qubit dephasing $\gamma/2\pi=116\pm7$ MHz and resonator decay $\kappa/2\pi=19.8\pm0.2$ MHz in the arXiv abstract.
