Demonstrates ancilla-free erasure conversion and mid-circuit erasure detection in a single integer fluxonium.

## Key Results

- Encodes logical states in $|g\rangle$ and $|f\rangle$, with $|e\rangle$ as the erasure state.
- Post-selection increased the $|f\rangle$ lifetime by $8.4\times$ and Hahn-echo time by $1.38\times$.
- Post-selected single-qubit gate error decreased from $0.061(2)\%$ to $0.030(5)\%$.
