Demonstrates a symmetric floating tunable-coupler architecture that combines fast adiabatic CZ control with an exact residual-$ZZ$ cancellation point at idle.

## Key Results
- Reports a 24 ns CZ fidelity of 99.927(15)%.
- An 8.5-hour stability run averaged 99.878(16)% CZ fidelity.
- Shows exact residual-$ZZ$ cancellation at an idle bias while retaining favorable adiabatic level ordering.
- Reports median CZ fidelity of 99.48% across 55 edges on 36-qubit devices, with 40–144 ns gate durations.
