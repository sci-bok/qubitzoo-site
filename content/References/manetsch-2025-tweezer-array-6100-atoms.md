Demonstrates a 6,100-atom optical tweezer array in ~12,000 sites (Caltech), a scale record for coherent neutral-atom qubits, with a 12.6(1) s hyperfine coherence time and routing operations characterized by interleaved randomized benchmarking.

## Key Results
- 6,100+ trapped atoms in ~12,000 tweezer sites; room-temperature lifetimes of 23 minutes.
- Record hyperfine-qubit coherence time of 12.6(1) s in a tweezer array.
- Imaging survival 99.98952(1)% with imaging fidelity > 99.99%.
- Coherence-preserving qubit transport and pick-up/drop-off demonstrated at large spatial scale via interleaved randomized benchmarking.
