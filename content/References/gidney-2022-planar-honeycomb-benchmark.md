Provides a detailed circuit-level benchmark of planar honeycomb Floquet memories, including thresholds and teraquop-style footprint estimates under several noise models.

## Key Results
- Planar patch optimization and boundary strategy for honeycomb memories
- Threshold estimates across SD6, SI1000, and EM3-style models
- Representative teraquop footprint: ~7000 qubits at 0.1% gate-level error (or ~900 with native pair measurements)
