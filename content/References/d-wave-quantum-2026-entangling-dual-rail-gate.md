A bias-preserving controlled-Z gate between two dual-rail cavity qubits implemented by a swap–wait–swap sequence through a transmon coupler.

## Key Results
- Gate duration of about 500 ns
- Erasure probability of about 0.5% per gate and residual Pauli errors below 0.1%
- Bit-flip errors at the $10^{-6}$ level while preserving the erasure-dominated hierarchy
