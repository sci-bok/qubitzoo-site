First experimental demonstration of a germanium hole-spin qubit. Shows that a Ge hut-wire double quantum dot can host a fast, all-electrically driven hole qubit with two-axis control.

## Key Results
- First single-hole germanium spin qubit demonstration
- Two-axis control via electric-dipole spin resonance in a double quantum dot
- Rabi frequencies up to 140 MHz
- Measured dephasing time $T_2^* \approx 130$ ns
- Highlights the role of spin-3/2 valence-band physics and strong spin-orbit coupling
