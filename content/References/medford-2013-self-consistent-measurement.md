This paper develops measurement, tomography, and single-shot readout methods for the three-spin exchange-only platform closely related to RX qubits. It is also the correct resolving destination for arXiv:1302.1933.

## Key Results
- Initialization, full electrical control, and single-shot readout of an exchange-only spin qubit
- Self-consistent state tomography with leakage-aware analysis
- Demonstrated dozens of qubit rotations in under 2 ns
