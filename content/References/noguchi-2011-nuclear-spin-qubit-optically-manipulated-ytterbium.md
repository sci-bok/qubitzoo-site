An early single-atom proof of principle for optically manipulated ytterbium nuclear-spin qubits, showing that the coherence-first idea was experimentally real long before the later tweezer-array scaling papers.

## Key Results
- Demonstrated full state tomography of a single ytterbium nuclear-spin qubit with fidelity above 0.98 and purity above 0.96.
- Achieved projective measurement in about 500 $\mu$s with efficiency about 0.98.
- Reported $T_1 = 0.49$ s and $T_2 = 0.10$ s.
- Positioned nuclear-spin qubits as a plausible route toward scalable neutral-atom architectures.
