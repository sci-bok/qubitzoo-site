Introduces a smooth-detuning trapped-ion entangling gate that adiabatically suppresses residual spin-motion entanglement and operates without motional ground-state cooling.

## Key Results
- Estimated two-qubit gate error: $8.4(7)\times10^{-5}$, corresponding to $99.9916(7)\%$ fidelity.
- Total smooth-gate duration in the benchmark: $225.8\,\mu\mathrm{s}$.
- Error remained $\lesssim5\times10^{-4}$ at mean phonon occupation up to $\bar n=9.4(3)$.
