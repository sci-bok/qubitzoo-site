Theory of radio-frequency SQUID and microwave-absorption observables intended to distinguish topological gap reopening from a dense background of trivial Andreev states in realistic wide planar junctions.

## Key Results
- Predicts a resonance-frequency-shift signature across the topological transition.
- Identifies a half-slope feature in microwave absorption.
- Explicitly treats the diagnostic ambiguity created by many trivial Andreev bound states.
