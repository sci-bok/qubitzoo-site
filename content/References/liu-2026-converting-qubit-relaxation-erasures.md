Demonstrates erasure conversion and detection within a single zero-flux fluxonium using its $|0\rangle$-$|2\rangle$ subspace.

## Key Results

- The same engineered resonator performs mid-circuit erasure detection and final logical readout.
- Post-selection on non-erasure outcomes increased logical lifetime from $193\,\mu\text{s}$ to $869\,\mu\text{s}$.
- Each erasure check contributed an inferred logical-dephasing error of $7.2\times10^{-5}$.
