Bao et al. demonstrated dipolar spin exchange and entanglement between individually trapped CaF molecules. This belongs to the ultracold polar-molecule branch rather than the chemically synthesized molecular-spin branch.

## Key Results
- Realized an effective spin-$1/2$ XY interaction in rotational states of tweezer-trapped CaF molecules.
- Generated a Bell state through an iSWAP-family operation with conditional fidelity $0.89(6)$.
- Demonstrated single-site molecular addressability with interleaved tweezer arrays.
