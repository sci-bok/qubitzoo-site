Develops shorter exchange-only gate constructions using simultaneous exchange and experimentally demonstrates a single-pulse X rotation.

## Key Results
- Replaces selected serial one-qubit sequences with simultaneous-exchange pulses.
- Demonstrates a single-pulse X rotation in a SiGe triple-dot device.
- Shows how simultaneous pulses can shorten subsequences inside encoded two-qubit gates.
