Reports beyond-break-even encoded computation on the 98-qubit Quantinuum Helios QCCD processor using high-rate iceberg codes.

## Key Results
- Demonstrated beyond-break-even encoded benchmarks with 48 to 94 logical qubits on Helios
- Used high-rate iceberg quantum error-detecting and concatenated iceberg quantum error-correcting codes on a dual-species Ba/Yb trapped-ion platform
- Showed encoded state preparation, measurement, logical-gate benchmarking, GHZ preparation, and partially fault-tolerant quantum simulation at beyond-classical scale
