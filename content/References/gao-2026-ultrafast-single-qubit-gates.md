Demonstrates ultrafast low-leakage control of a weakly anharmonic transmon by suppressing higher-order multiphoton transitions with recursively corrected DRAG pulses.

## Key Results
- $X$ and $X/2$ gates as short as 6.8 ns with fidelities above 99.98%.
- Total leakage error consistently below $2.0\times10^{-5}$.
- Amplified leakage-error metrology resolves coherent leakage near the $10^{-6}$ level.
- Demonstrated on a 5.323 GHz transmon with $-225$ MHz anharmonicity.
