Landmark entangling-gate paper for germanium hole-spin qubits. Demonstrates that strong spin-orbit-enabled, all-electrical hole qubits can also support fast exchange-based two-qubit logic.

## Key Results
- First fast two-qubit logic demonstration with holes in germanium
- Exchange-based entangling operations in a Ge quantum-dot device
- Reported two-qubit gate fidelity of 98%
- Established hole-spin qubits as a realistic multiqubit processor platform rather than only a single-qubit modality
