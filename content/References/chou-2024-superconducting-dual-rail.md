Experimental dual-rail qubit encoded in the single-photon subspace of two superconducting cavities, with projective logical measurement integrated with erasure detection.

## Key Results
- Logical state preparation and measurement error at the 0.01% level
- More than 99% of cavity-decay events detected as erasures
- Decay errors near 0.2% per microsecond, phase errors six times lower, and bit flips at least 140 times lower
