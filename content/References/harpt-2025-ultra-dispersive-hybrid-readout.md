Demonstrates quantum-dot hybrid-qubit readout through a parametrically activated longitudinal interaction with a superconducting resonator.

## Key Results
- Operates with qubit-resonator detuning of roughly 10 GHz in an ultra-dispersive regime.
- Uses state-dependent resonator response near the hybrid-qubit anticrossings.
- Reconstructs the four-state double-dot Hamiltonian from spectroscopy and readout data.
