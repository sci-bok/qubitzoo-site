## Abstract

A soft-transmon cos(2φ) device demonstrates coherent control, single-shot readout, and strong suppression of charge-induced relaxation while exposing residual flux noise as the dominant limitation.

## Key Results

- Resolved an opposite-Cooper-pair-parity doublet split by 13.6 MHz.
- Measured a 100-fold suppression of the island charge matrix element relative to the unprotected plasmon transition.
- Reported $T_1=70\,\mu\mathrm{s}$ and $T_2^{\mathrm{echo}}=2.5\,\mu\mathrm{s}$, both limited by $1/f$ flux noise.
