Establishes the fundamental added-noise limits for phase-insensitive and phase-sensitive linear amplifiers. In the high-gain phase-insensitive case, input-referred added noise is at least one-half quantum.

## Key Results
- Derives the half-quantum added-noise limit for high-gain phase-insensitive amplification
- Distinguishes phase-insensitive from phase-sensitive quantum limits
