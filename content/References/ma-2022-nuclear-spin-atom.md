A direct demonstration that $^{171}$Yb nuclear-spin qubits can support a universal gate toolkit in optical tweezers, combining long-lived storage with high-fidelity single-qubit control and Rydberg-mediated entangling operations.

## Key Results
- Implemented initialization, readout, and single-qubit gates on $^{171}$Yb nuclear-spin qubits trapped at a 486.78 nm magic wavelength.
- Reported $T_1 \approx 20$ s, $T_2^* = 1.24(5)$ s, and single-qubit gate fidelity $0.99959(6)$.
- Demonstrated Rydberg-blockade entangling gates and coherent control of those gate operations via 369 nm light shifts on the Yb$^+$ ion-core transition.
- Marked the shift from coherence-only nuclear-spin demos to a full gate-based computing architecture.
