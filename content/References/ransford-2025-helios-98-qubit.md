Describes Quantinuum Helios, a 98-qubit QCCD trapped-ion quantum computer built around a rotatable ion storage ring and junction-connected operation zones. Published in Nature 655, 81-86 (2026); arXiv preprint 2511.05465 (2025).

## Key Results
- 98-qubit trapped-ion QCCD processor using $^{137}$Ba$^+$ hyperfine data qubits
- All-to-all connectivity enabled by a rotatable ion storage ring coupled to two operation regions by a junction
- Average infidelities of $2.5(1)\times10^{-5}$ for single-qubit gates, $7.9(2)\times10^{-4}$ for two-qubit gates, and $4.8(6)\times10^{-4}$ for SPAM
- Qubit defined on the $|F=1,m_F=0\rangle \leftrightarrow |F=2,m_F=0\rangle$ clock transition at a 3.95 G bias field (second-order Zeeman coefficient 488.8 Hz/G²)
