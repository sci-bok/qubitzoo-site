Describes Quantinuum Helios, a 98-qubit QCCD trapped-ion quantum computer built around a rotatable ion storage ring and junction-connected operation zones.

## Key Results
- 98-qubit trapped-ion QCCD processor using $^{137}$Ba$^+$ hyperfine data qubits
- All-to-all connectivity enabled by a rotatable ion storage ring coupled to two operation regions by a junction
- Average infidelities of $2.5\times10^{-5}$ for single-qubit gates, $7.9\times10^{-4}$ for two-qubit gates, and $4.8\times10^{-4}$ for SPAM
