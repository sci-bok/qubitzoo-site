Establishes metastable trapped-ion qubits as an experimental erasure platform with integrated detection during entangling operations.

## Key Results
- Detects about $94\%$ of spontaneous Raman-scattering errors and nearly all qubit-decay errors
- Two-ion geometric-phase gate with $98.61\%$ SPAM-corrected Bell-state fidelity
- Fidelity increases to $99.16\%$ after removing detected erasures
