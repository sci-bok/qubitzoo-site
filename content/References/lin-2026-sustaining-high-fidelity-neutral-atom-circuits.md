Neutral-atom experiment integrating mid-circuit cooling, re-initialization, and non-destructive erasure detection to sustain high-fidelity controlled logic across repeated operational rounds.

## Key Results
- Raw controlled-gate fidelity $99.60(1)\%$, increased to $99.81(1)\%$ with non-destructive erasure detection
- Roughly $99.8\%$ gate performance maintained across repeated rounds
- In-circuit Raman sideband cooling and qubit re-initialization address accumulated motional heating and atom loss
