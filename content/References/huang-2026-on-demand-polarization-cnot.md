Demonstrated a postselected two-photon polarization CNOT using on-demand photons from a mesa-top quantum dot. The published result reported $F_{ZZ}=0.902\pm0.043$, process-fidelity bounds of 0.776–0.874, and Bell-state fidelity $0.825\pm0.010$.
