Open-microcavity enhancement of a single NV center's optical interface for higher-rate spin-photon networking.

## Key Results
- Increased the zero-phonon-line fraction from the native ~3% to more than 44% with a Purcell factor of about 1.8
- Demonstrated cavity-assisted resonance fluorescence without temporal filtering
- Reported a signal-to-laser background ratio above 10 and projected more than an order-of-magnitude improvement in spin-spin entanglement success probability
