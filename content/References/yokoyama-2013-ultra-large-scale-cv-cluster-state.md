## Summary

Demonstrates deterministic time-domain multiplexing of individually addressable optical wavepackets into an ultra-large continuous-variable cluster state. The experiment established temporal multiplexing as a practical route to cluster-state scaling and reported more than 10,000 modes in the original paper; later extended operation supported the widely cited million-mode scale.
