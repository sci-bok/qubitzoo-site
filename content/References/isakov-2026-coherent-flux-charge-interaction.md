Demonstrates a drive-induced flux-charge interaction in a gate-tunable semiconductor-junction fluxonium, using parity selection rules to separate the interaction from ordinary capacitive driving.

## Key Results

- Gate modulation coherently drove the even-parity $|0\rangle\rightarrow|2\rangle$ transition at half flux, where purely capacitive excitation is forbidden.
- The charge-insensitive-with-variable-$E_J$ (ChIVE) point retained finite flux-charge coupling while making the transition frequency first-order insensitive to gate-voltage noise.
- At the ChIVE point, the $|0\rangle\rightarrow|2\rangle$ transition was 3.61 GHz; the supplementary measurements reported $T_1=6.62\,\mu\mathrm{s}$ and Ramsey $T_2=144$ ns for that transition.
