Demonstrates high-fidelity individual and parallel single-qubit control in a five-qubit silicon spin array using tailored pulses and pairwise calibration of microwave-drive-induced AC Stark shifts.

## Key Results
- Primitive $X$ and $Y$ gate fidelities above $99.99\%$ for all five qubits when operated individually, with some approaching $99.999\%$.
- Interleaved-RB $X_{\pi/2}$ fidelity of $99.9985(8)\%$ and $Y_{\pi/2}$ fidelity of $99.995(1)\%$ on a representative qubit.
- Simultaneous operation of up to three qubits retained primitive-gate fidelities above $99.99\%$; five-qubit parallel operation remained above $99.9\%$.
