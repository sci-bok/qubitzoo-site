## Citation
Y.-H. Wu, L. C. Camenzind, P. Bütler et al., "Simultaneous high-fidelity single-qubit gates in a spin qubit array," [Nature Physics (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-026-03466-2) · [arXiv:2507.11918](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.11918).

## Key Results
- Demonstrated primitive 1Q gate fidelities above 99.99% across all five qubits in a $^{28}$Si/SiGe array, with some approaching 99.999%
- Preserved >99.99% fidelity during simultaneous operation of up to three qubits and ≥99.9% during five-qubit parallel operation
- Identified microwave-drive-induced a.c.-Stark shifts as the dominant parallel-control error and compensated them with pairwise phase calibrations
