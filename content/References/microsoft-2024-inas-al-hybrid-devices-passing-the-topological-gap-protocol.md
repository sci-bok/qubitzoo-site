Reports gate-defined InAs-Al hybrid devices that pass the topological gap protocol, a structured sequence of local and non-local transport checks designed to reduce false positives from trivial near-zero modes. It is one of the strongest protocol-based experimental claims in the modern Majorana nanowire literature, but it is still not a demonstration of protected logical-qubit operation.

## Key Results
- Several gate-defined InAs-Al devices reported to pass the topological gap protocol
- Simultaneous local and non-local transport signatures consistent with a topological phase over finite parameter windows
- Estimated topological gaps in the roughly 20-60 micro-eV range
- Frames the result as a prerequisite for later fusion and braiding experiments, not as a completed logical-qubit demonstration
