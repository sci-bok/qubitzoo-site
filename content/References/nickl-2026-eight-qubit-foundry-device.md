## Citation
A. Nickl et al., "Eight-qubit operation of a 300 mm SiMOS foundry-fabricated device," [Nature Communications **17**, 5878 (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-74597-6) · [arXiv:2512.10174](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.10174).

## Key Results
- Tuned and coherently controlled an eight-spin linear array fabricated in a 300-mm SiMOS foundry process
- Reported $T_2^*$ up to 41(2) μs and Hahn-echo coherence up to 1.31(4) ms
- Demonstrated cascaded readout of the central four qubits and exchange control in one double-dot pair
- Did not yet demonstrate a high-fidelity entangling gate across the eight-qubit array
