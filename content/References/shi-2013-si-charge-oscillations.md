Demonstrates coherent charge oscillations and echo in a Si/SiGe double quantum dot, establishing the charge-qubit dynamics of the silicon platform and quantifying its sensitivity to detuning noise.

## Key Results
- Measured $T_2^*$ values from $127\,\text{ps}$ to approximately $2.1\,\text{ns}$.
- Extended a $127\,\text{ps}$ free-induction decay to $760\,\text{ps}$ using charge echo.
- Connected the operating-point dependence to low-frequency detuning noise.
