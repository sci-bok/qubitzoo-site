Demonstrates a tunable Josephson metamaterial amplifier operating from 4 to 8 GHz. In phase-sensitive operation it added less than half a noise quantum and squeezed vacuum fluctuations by 10 dB.

## Key Results
- 4–8 GHz tunable operating range
- Less than one-half quantum of added noise in phase-sensitive operation
- 10 dB squeezing of quantum fluctuations
