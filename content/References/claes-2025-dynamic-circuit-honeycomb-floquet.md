Shows that a dynamic-circuit implementation can materially improve simulated honeycomb Floquet performance, including higher timelike distance and reduced qubit footprint at fixed logical target.

## Key Results
- Dynamic-circuit honeycomb protocol with no ancilla-mediated stabilizer extraction
- Improved timelike distance and leakage handling in simulation
- Estimated ~3× qubit-count reduction at physical error rate 1e-3 for a 1e-12 logical-error target
