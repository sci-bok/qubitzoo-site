First systematic gate benchmarking and two-qubit operation in a two-nanowire-gatemon circuit.

## Key Results

- Randomized-benchmarking single-qubit errors below 0.7% for all tested gates, including voltage-controlled $Z$ rotations.
- Coherent capacitive coupling and swap operations between two gatemons.
- Estimated controlled-phase-gate fidelity of 91%.
