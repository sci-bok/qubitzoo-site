Huang et al. used tip-assisted assembly and ESR-STM to create and coherently control an FePc–Fe(C$_6$H$_6$) mixed-spin ferrimagnet with a well-separated ground-state doublet.

## Key Results
- Realized a correlated mixed-spin $(1/2,1)$ molecular ferrimagnet on MgO/Ag(001).
- Measured a single-molecule spin lifetime up to $T_1=1.6\,\mu\text{s}$.
- Demonstrated that assembled ferrimagnet units can support ferromagnetic or antiferromagnetic intermolecular coupling.
