Studies junction and surface two-level systems in flux-tunable Al/AlO$_x$/Al mergemons. The best design cohort reached mean $T_1$ values up to 130 μs ($Q\approx3.3\times10^6$). Geometry sweeps showed that exposed-surface participation can remain a major limitation even when junction energy participation exceeds 90%.

## Key Results
- Mean $T_1$ up to 130 μs in flux-tunable devices
- Junction energy participation above 90% across the studied designs
- Strain and electric-field spectroscopy used to distinguish TLS classes
- Surface-loss mitigation remains necessary in the merged geometry
