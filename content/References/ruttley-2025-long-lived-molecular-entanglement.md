Demonstrates long-lived RbCs molecular entanglement in magic-wavelength optical tweezers, with raw Bell-state fidelity $0.924^{+0.013}_{-0.016}$ and leakage-corrected fidelity $0.976^{+0.014}_{-0.016}$.
