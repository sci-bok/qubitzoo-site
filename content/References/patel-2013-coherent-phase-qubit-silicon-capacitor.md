This paper used a suspended single-crystal silicon shunt capacitor to reduce dielectric loss in a Josephson phase qubit.

## Key Results
- Measured energy-relaxation times up to $T_1=1.6\,\mu\mathrm{s}$
- Measured $T_2^*=110$ ns at a 5.093 GHz operating point
- Demonstrated more than a factor-of-two $T_1$ improvement over amorphous-shunt phase qubits
