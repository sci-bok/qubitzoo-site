First experimental universal control and process tomography of the Si/SiGe quantum-dot hybrid qubit.

## Key Results
- Demonstrates sub-100-ps rotations about two Bloch-sphere axes in a three-electron double dot.
- Reports process fidelities of 85-89% for the measured $X$ rotations and 94-96% for the measured $Z$ rotations.
- Measures $T_2^*\approx2$ ns in the charge-like regime and $T_2^*\approx10$ ns for spin-like phase evolution.
