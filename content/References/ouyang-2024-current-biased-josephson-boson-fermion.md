This experiment revisited the bias-controlled spectrum of a quantized current-biased Josephson junction, observing a crossover from nearly harmonic-oscillator behavior at low bias to a strongly nonlinear two-level artificial-atom regime near critical current.

## Key Results
- Fabricated and characterized current-biased Josephson-junction devices at 50 mK
- Observed a 2.5949 GHz plasma resonance with $Q=507$ at zero bias
- Confirmed strong spectral nonlinearity near $I_\mathrm{bias}/I_c=0.97$
