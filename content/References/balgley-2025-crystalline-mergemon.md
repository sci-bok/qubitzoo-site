Demonstrates vertically stacked NbSe$_2$/WSe$_2$/NbSe$_2$ Josephson junctions and a prototype all-crystalline merged-element transmon. Josephson coupling persisted across 2–12 nm of WSe$_2$, and the prototype showed dispersive coupling to a microwave resonator.

## Key Results
- Crystalline WSe$_2$ weak links spanning 3–18 atomic layers
- Prototype matched its designed transmon frequency and anharmonicity
- Dispersive qubit-resonator coupling demonstrated
- Coherent time-domain control was not yet established in this report
