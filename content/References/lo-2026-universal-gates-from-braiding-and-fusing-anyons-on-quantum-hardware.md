A processor demonstration that promotes fusion and anyon-charge measurement to computational primitives, making the minimally non-Abelian $D(S_3)$ order universal.

## Key Results

- Prepared a 54-qubit $D(S_3)$ topological wavefunction on Quantinuum H2.
- Encoded logical qutrits in global fusion space and implemented a universal gate set using braiding plus fusion measurement.
- Demonstrated readout and topological magic-state preparation.
