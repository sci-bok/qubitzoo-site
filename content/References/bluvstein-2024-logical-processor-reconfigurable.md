Logical neutral-atom processor milestone combining reconfigurable atom arrays, logical control, and high-fidelity Rydberg-mediated entangling gates.

## Key Results
- Realized a programmable logical quantum processor with up to 280 physical atoms and 48 logical qubits.
- Combined reconfigurable zoning, high-fidelity two-qubit gates, single-qubit rotations, and mid-circuit readout.
- Reported 99.97% single-qubit fidelity, 99.5% two-qubit CZ fidelity, and 1–4 s hyperfine coherence.
- Made neutral atoms a serious logical-computing platform rather than only an analog-simulation platform.
