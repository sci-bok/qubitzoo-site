Uses gate-set tomography to characterize microwave operations on a single trapped $^{171}$Yb$^+$ hyperfine qubit. The title-page author order was verified against the paper PDF.

## Key Results
- Diamond-norm errors of $(1.58\pm0.15)\times10^{-4}$, $(1.39\pm0.22)\times10^{-4}$, and $(1.62\pm0.27)\times10^{-4}$ for idle, $X_{\pi/2}$, and $Y_{\pi/2}$ operations
- All three operations passed the cited $6.7\times10^{-4}$ rigorous fault-tolerance threshold with greater than 95% confidence
