Refines the ground-state $^{171}$Yb$^+$ magnetic constants used to evaluate the microwave clock transition. The title-page author order was verified against the arXiv PDF.

## Key Results
- Electronic Landé factor $g_J=2.002615(70)$
- First-order Zeeman coefficient 14,010.78(49) Hz/$\mu$T
- Second-order clock-transition coefficient 31.0869(22) mHz/$\mu$T$^2$ = 310.869(22) Hz/G$^2$
