Benchmarks a time-optimal Rydberg controlled-$Z$ gate and develops a response theory that connects gate infidelity to measured laser-noise spectra.

## Key Results
- CZ fidelity averaged over symmetric input states: $0.9971(5)$.
- The reported value is downward-corrected for leakage and is not a full two-qubit Haar-average fidelity.
- The validated error model identifies laser-frequency noise, Rydberg decay, and motion as leading contributions.
