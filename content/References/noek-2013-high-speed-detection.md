Demonstrates direct fluorescence detection of a $^{171}$Yb$^+$ hyperfine qubit using high photon-collection efficiency, low background, and time-resolved photon counting. The title-page author order and DOI were reverified during the 2026-11-07 deep audit.

## Key Results
- Detection fidelities of 99%, 99.85(1)%, and 99.915(7)% at average detection times of 10.5, 28.1, and 99.8 $\mu$s
- Direct bright/dark fluorescence discrimination; this experiment did not use electron shelving
