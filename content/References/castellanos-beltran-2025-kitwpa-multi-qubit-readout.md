Reports direct integration of a kinetic-inductance traveling-wave parametric amplifier into a multiplexed multi-qubit readout chain and shows measurable improvement in system noise, SNR, and qubit-state discrimination relative to a HEMT-first chain.

## Key Results
- Demonstrates KI-TWPA deployment in a real multiplexed qubit-readout setup rather than a standalone amplifier benchmark.
- Quantifies system-noise reduction through an ac-Stark-shift calibration at the qubit chip reference plane.
- Shows measurable improvement in readout SNR and state discrimination over a HEMT-first chain.
- Important evidence that KI-TWPAs are now practical readout hardware, not just promising component demos.
