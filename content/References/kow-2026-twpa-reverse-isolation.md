Demonstrates a multistage traveling-wave parametric amplifier that combines near-quantum-limited forward gain with passive in-band reverse isolation.

## Key Results
- 20 dB forward gain across 1.6 GHz
- More than 35 dB passive reverse isolation
- Noise performance at 1.7 times the quantum limit
