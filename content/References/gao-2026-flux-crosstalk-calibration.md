Demonstrates indirect, high-precision calibration of flux crosstalk affecting tunable couplers without dedicated coupler drive or readout channels.

## Key Results
- Introduces zero-knowledge characterization using intrinsic coupler-resonator shifts before detailed qubit or coupler spectroscopy.
- Introduces symmetric-branch cancellation to separate target-coupler crosstalk from neighboring dispersive effects.
- Suppresses residual coupler flux crosstalk below $5\times10^{-5}$ after iterative compensation.
