Materials-and-device milestone for Microsoft's InAs-Al Majorana program. Reports hybrid devices that pass the topological gap protocol, providing evidence consistent with a topological regime and establishing the device quality needed before tetron-style fusion or parity-readout experiments.

## Key Results
- Several devices pass the topological gap protocol.
- Extracted topological gaps are reported in the 20-60 $\mu$eV range.
- Establishes a prerequisite materials/device milestone, not a demonstrated logical tetron qubit.
