Simulation study of parallel one- and two-qubit gates in four-qubit flip-flop arrays under realistic $1/f$ electric-field noise.

## Key Results

- Compared linear, square, and star layouts for parallel operations.
- Found that simultaneous two-qubit gates are substantially more vulnerable to spectator and geometry effects than one-qubit operations.
- Reported an average modeled $i\mathrm{SWAP}$ infidelity increase from roughly 0.02 for one operation to 0.2 for two parallel operations in the studied conditions.
