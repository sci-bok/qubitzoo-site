Demonstrates hardware-efficient, symmetric dispersive readout of a transmon dual-rail qubit with fast single-shot and continuous erasure detection.

## Key Results
- $384\,\mathrm{ns}$ single-shot erasure detection
- Residual error $6.0(2)\times10^{-4}$ and induced dephasing $8(3)\times10^{-5}$ per check
- Continuous detection during one-qubit gates with median gate error $7.2\times10^{-5}$ and less than $10^{-5}$ added by detection
