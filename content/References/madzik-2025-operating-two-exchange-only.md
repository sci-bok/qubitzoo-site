Demonstrates simultaneous operation of two neighboring exchange-only qubits in a six-dot segment of an Intel Tunnel Falls device.

## Key Results
- First experimental exchange-only iSWAP and charge-locking Pauli-spin-blockade readout.
- Sequential and parallelized two-qubit Clifford fidelities: 96.25 ± 0.07% and 95.80 ± 0.08%.
- Parallel scheduling reduces two-qubit gate duration by about 40%.
- Sequential interleaved fidelities: CNOT 97.55 ± 0.47%, iSWAP 97.43 ± 0.43%, SWAP 99.03 ± 0.33%.
