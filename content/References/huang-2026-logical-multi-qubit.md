Four coupled-transmon dual-rail erasure qubits integrated in a superconducting processor, extending the platform from protected single-qubit operation to universal logical control and multi-qubit entanglement.

## Key Results
- Logical Bell-state fidelity: 98.8%
- Logical CNOT process fidelity: 98.1% at a 13% erasure rate
- Three-logical-qubit GHZ-state fidelity: 93.9%
- Archival Nature Physics values supersede the earlier preprint values
