First demonstration of coherent manipulation of an Andreev spin qubit: a qubit encoded in the spin degree of freedom of a single Andreev bound state in a semiconductor-superconductor Josephson junction. Distinct from the gatemon (which uses the charge/parity degree of freedom).

## Key Results
- Qubit states: the odd-parity spin doublet of a single Andreev bound state
- Coherent control via Raman transitions through an auxiliary Andreev state, with Ramsey and Hahn-echo measurements
- Best reported spin lifetime: $T_s = 17 \pm 1\,\mu$s
- Coherence times: $T_{2R} = 18 \pm 1$ ns and $T_{2E} = 52 \pm 3$ ns
- Single-shot readout via the dispersive shift of a coupled microwave resonator
- Platform: InAs/Al nanowire Josephson junction
