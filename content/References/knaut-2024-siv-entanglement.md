Demonstrates entanglement between two SiV⁻ quantum-memory nodes using bidirectional 737 nm ↔ 1350 nm conversion, including 40 km fiber spools and a 35 km deployed Boston-area fiber loop. Author order was verified against the arXiv PDF title page.

## Key Results
- Electron-spin Bell fidelities: $0.86(3)$ and $0.74(3)$, with entanglement persisting at success rates up to 1 Hz
- Error-detected nuclear-spin Bell fidelity: $0.77(5)$; entanglement storage up to 1 s
- Deployed-loop nuclear-spin Bell fidelity: $0.69(7)$
- Distance: 35 km deployed fiber loop (17 dB loss); entanglement through 40 km fiber spools
- Quantum frequency conversion: 737 nm → 1350 nm telecom band
