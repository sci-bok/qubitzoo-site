Showed that $^{171}$Yb nuclear-spin qubits can support repetitive projective readout and real-time feedforward, which is exactly the kind of capability needed for adaptive circuits and QEC.

## Key Results
- Achieved state-discrimination fidelity 0.993(4) for nuclear-spin projective measurement.
- Reported state-averaged readout survival 0.994(3).
- Demonstrated repeated measurements in different bases, quantum-Zeno behavior, and measurement-conditioned state preparation.
- Strengthened the case that nuclear-spin neutral atoms are not just long-lived memories, but viable mid-circuit-control qubits.
