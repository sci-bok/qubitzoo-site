Proposes a quartic-superinductor version of blochnium that keeps the quasicharge-protection logic of the original device while increasing anharmonicity and reducing leakage and frequency-crowding concerns.

## Key Results
- Identifies ordinary blochnium as protected but only weakly anharmonic, with transmon-like crowding concerns in multi-qubit settings.
- Proposes a quartic superinductor chain to boost anharmonicity without giving up the quasicharge operating regime.
- Frames quartic blochnium as a design follow-on, not a newer experimental record.
