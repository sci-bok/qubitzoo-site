Introduces a Floquet-Bacon-Shor measurement schedule that preserves the usual static logical qubit while creating additional dynamical logical qubits protected by the time-periodic check pattern.

## Key Results
- Period-4 measurement schedule for Bacon-Shor gauge checks
- Additional dynamical logical qubits on the same $d\times d$ lattice
- Distance scaling that can saturate the subsystem bound more efficiently than static Bacon-Shor
- Shows that some errors are corrected by the schedule itself
