Demonstrates local bipartite entanglement inside a fully connected three-$^{13}$C nuclear-spin register coupled to a highly strained SiV⁻ electron spin. Author order was verified against the PDF title page.

## Key Results
- High strain enables SiV⁻ operation near 4 K with electron-spin $T_1=0.296(85)$ s
- Conditional electron-spin phase gate mediates nuclear-nuclear entanglement
- Direct microwave and radio-frequency control of the local register
