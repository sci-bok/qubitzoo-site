Demonstrates quantum control of a dissipatively stabilized superconducting cat qubit while preserving its intrinsic bit-flip protection.

## Key Results
- Bit-flip time exceeding 10 s, roughly four orders of magnitude beyond earlier controlled cat-qubit implementations
- Phase-flip time above 490 ns
- Preparation, imaging, and phase control of quantum superpositions without destroying bit-flip protection
