Demonstrates a stable direct CNOT gate between two inductively coupled fluxonium qubits using selective darkening.

## Key Results

- A 60 ns direct CNOT reached $99.94\%$ randomized-benchmarking fidelity.
- Fidelity remained above $99.9\%$ for 24 days without recalibration.
- The paired 60 ns identity gate reached $99.96\%$, constraining nondecoherence-related gate error to about $2\times10^{-4}$.
