Demonstrates creation and coherent mapping of many-particle entangled states across distinct qubit degrees of freedom (nuclear-spin, optical-clock, and Rydberg) in ¹⁷¹Yb tweezer arrays, including a 20-atom GHZ state transferred between manifolds and error-detected two-qubit gates at 99.78(4)%.

## Key Results
- Coherently transfers up to 20 atoms of a Z₂-ordered GHZ state from the Rydberg manifold to the metastable nuclear-spin manifold in a 2D ladder geometry.
- Error-detected two-qubit gate fidelity of 99.78(4)% in metastable qubits via atom-loss-detectable measurement and delayed erasure detection.
- >90% Rydberg decay detection enables mid-circuit erasure detection and an effective Rydberg-qubit lifetime of 1.2(2) ms.
- Establishes ¹⁷¹Yb as a bridge architecture between computing, simulation, and metrology qubit platforms.
