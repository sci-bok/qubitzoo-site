Demonstrates high-fidelity AEON control with simultaneous two-exchange pulses in a triangular Si/SiGe quantum-dot array.

## Key Results
- Average one-qubit Clifford fidelity: 99.86%.
- Leakage error: 0.015% per Clifford.
- Average AEON Clifford depth: 1.9 simultaneous-exchange pulses.
