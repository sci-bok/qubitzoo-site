Demonstrates a leakage-protected idle point created by equal, simultaneous three-way exchange in a triangular exchange-only qubit.

## Key Results
- Equal couplings create a gap (E_g=3J/2) between the (S=1/2) logical and (S=3/2) leakage manifolds.
- The exchange operation is an identity on the logical qubit up to phase.
- Measured dephasing outperforms conventional exchange-off idling for (E_g/h<60) MHz.
