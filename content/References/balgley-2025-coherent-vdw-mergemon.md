Demonstrates coherent, fully crystalline NbSe$_2$/WSe$_2$/NbSe$_2$ merged-element transmons without external shunt capacitors. The first-generation devices reached microsecond-scale lifetimes; the best-performing device had mean $T_1=1.67\pm0.27$ μs and best observed $T_1=2.46$ μs. Comparison with structurally similar capacitors identified dielectric loss as the dominant relaxation channel.

## Key Results
- Coherent time-domain control in fully van der Waals mergemons
- Best mean $T_1=1.67\pm0.27$ μs; best observed $T_1=2.46$ μs
- Junction participation of 96% for the best-performing device
- Dielectric loss remained dominant despite crystalline materials
