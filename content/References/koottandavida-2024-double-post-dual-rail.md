Experimental dual-rail qubit encoded in the symmetric and antisymmetric modes of a compact double-post superconducting cavity, with an auxiliary transmon used for erasure detection and joint-Wigner tomography.

## Key Results
- Erasure rate: $3.981(3)\,\mathrm{ms}^{-1}$
- Residual postselected dephasing rate: up to $0.17\,\mathrm{ms}^{-1}$
- Demonstrates a compact hardware-efficient cavity realization with a strong erasure-versus-dephasing hierarchy
