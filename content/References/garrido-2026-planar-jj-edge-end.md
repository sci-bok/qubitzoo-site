Theory of end-like and edge-like Majorana localization in planar Josephson junctions with Rashba and Dresselhaus spin-orbit coupling, including tunability by field strength, field direction, phase difference, and crystal orientation.

## Key Results
- Distinguishes endpoint-localized and edge-extended Majorana regimes.
- Shows that field and phase can tune between localization classes.
- Analyzes disorder and proposes edge-like modes as possible interconnects.
