First coherent circuit-QED realization of a single electron trapped above solid neon. It demonstrated $g/2\pi=3.5\,\mathrm{MHz}$ strong electron-photon coupling, microwave control, dispersive readout, $T_1=15\,\mu\text{s}$, and Hahn-echo coherence $T_2^{\mathrm{echo}}=220\,\mathrm{ns}$.
