First experimental realization of strong circuit QED, coupling a Cooper pair box to a superconducting CPW resonator strongly enough to resolve the vacuum-Rabi doublet.

## Key Results
- Resonator frequency: 6.04 GHz.
- Vacuum-Rabi splitting: about 11.6 MHz, corresponding to $g/2\pi\approx5.8$ MHz.
- Cavity loss $\kappa/2\pi=0.8$ MHz and qubit decoherence $\gamma/2\pi=0.7$ MHz, satisfying strong coupling.
