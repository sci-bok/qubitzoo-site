## Abstract

A gate-tunable semiconductor Josephson interferometer generates a dominant cos(2φ) term near half a flux quantum, realizing a Cooper-pair-parity-protected qubit.

## Key Results

- Demonstrated a tenfold suppression of qubit relaxation when tuned into the protected regime.
- Established the first experimental parity-protection milestone for a cos(2φ) qubit architecture.
- Showed that nanowire-junction tunability can compensate interferometer imbalance.
