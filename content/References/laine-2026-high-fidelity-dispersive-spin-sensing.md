This work integrates a compact radio-frequency single-electron-box sensor into a bilinear silicon-MOS quantum-dot unit cell fabricated with an industrial 300-mm process.

## Abstract

Independent control of sensor and double-dot tunnel rates enables 99.92% parity-readout fidelity in 340 μs and 99% in 20 μs. A hidden-Markov model accounts for the three relevant two-electron spin states and improves single-shot classification. The result shows that compact dispersive sensing can preserve qubit connectivity without sacrificing state-of-the-art sensitivity.

## Key Findings

- 99.92% dispersive parity-readout fidelity in 340 μs.
- 99% fidelity in 20 μs.
- Compact single-electron-box sensing in an industrial 300-mm silicon-MOS process.
