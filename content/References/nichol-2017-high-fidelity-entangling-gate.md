GaAs demonstration of a high-fidelity capacitive entangling gate between two $S$–$T_0$ double-dot qubits using large magnetic-field gradients to reduce charge-noise sensitivity.

## Key Results
- Approximately 99% single-qubit gate fidelity.
- 90% entangling-gate fidelity from self-consistent measurement, state, and process tomography.
