This preprint reports lattice-surgery operations on a pair of distance-3 superconducting surface-code logical qubits.

## Key Findings

- Implemented merge/split operations with repeated syndrome extraction and prepared an error-corrected logical Bell state.
- Executed a two-logical-qubit Deutsch–Jozsa circuit.
- Used magic-state injection and gate teleportation to realize logical non-Clifford rotations, with conditioned $R_X(\pi/4)$ fidelity $0.943^{+0.010}_{-0.009}$.
