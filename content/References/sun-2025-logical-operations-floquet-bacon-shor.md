Experimental realization of a 3×3 Floquet-Bacon-Shor code on a superconducting processor, including repeated error detection, logical gates on a dynamical logical qubit, and entanglement between static and dynamical logical qubits.

## Key Results
- Realizes a Floquet-Bacon-Shor code with one static and one dynamical logical qubit
- Demonstrates fault-tolerant encoding and measurement of logical two-qubit states
- Demonstrates universal single-qubit logical gates on the dynamical qubit
- Reports an error-detected logical Bell-state fidelity of 75.9%
