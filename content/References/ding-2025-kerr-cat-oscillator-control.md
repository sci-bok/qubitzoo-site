Demonstrates driven conditional-displacement coupling between a Kerr-cat ancilla and a high-Q microwave cavity.

## Key Results
- Conditional displacement plus Kerr-cat one-qubit gates enables universal cavity control
- Frequency-selective dissipation suppresses heating-induced cavity dephasing
- Measured Kerr strength K/2pi = 0.93(3) MHz in the demonstrated device
