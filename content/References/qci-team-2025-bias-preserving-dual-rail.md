Experimental two-qubit entangling gate for cavity dual-rail erasure qubits. The displayed paper title page credits the Quantum Circuits, Inc. Team, although the arXiv metadata lists individual authors beginning with Nitish Mehta.

## Key Results
- Gate duration approximately 500 ns
- Residual gate infidelity below 0.1% after erasure detection
- Erasure probability approximately 0.5% per gate
- Bit-flip errors bounded at the few-parts-per-million level
