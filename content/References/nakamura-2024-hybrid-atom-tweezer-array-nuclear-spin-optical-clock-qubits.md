Demonstrated a hybrid dual-isotope ytterbium tweezer array that treats $^{171}$Yb nuclear-spin qubits as long-lived data qubits and $^{174}$Yb optical-clock qubits as ancillas, sharpening the QEC story for alkaline-earth neutral atoms.

## Key Results
- Realized a dual-isotope array combining $^{171}$Yb nuclear-spin data qubits with $^{174}$Yb optical-clock ancilla qubits.
- Measured 99.1(1.8)% retained nuclear-spin coherence during 20 ms ancilla imaging.
- Reported ancilla imaging fidelity 0.9992 and survival probability 0.988.
- Made the data-qubit / ancilla separation explicit instead of forcing one manifold to handle storage, gates, and readout simultaneously.
