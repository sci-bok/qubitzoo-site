A 27-transmon digital realization of Fibonacci string-net order and non-Abelian braiding on a superconducting processor.

## Key Results

- Prepared a Fibonacci string-net state with average vertex and plaquette expectations of 0.94 and 0.58.
- Created, braided, and fused Fibonacci anyons and measured $\phi_{\mathrm{exp}}=1.60$, close to the ideal quantum dimension $1.618$.
- Explicitly distinguished circuit-compiled braiding from an intrinsically gap-protected adiabatic braid.
