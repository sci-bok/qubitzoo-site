HgTe/Al planar-junction experiment in which a zero-bias tunnelling feature appeared over a phase interval that broadened with in-plane magnetic field, consistent with a phase-controlled topological transition.

## Key Results
- Phase difference and in-plane field jointly control the zero-bias region.
- Tunnelling conductance is measured at the junction edge.
- The observations are consistent with modelled Majorana-bound-state phenomenology but do not establish non-Abelian statistics.
