## Citation
B. Undseth, N. Meggiato, Y.-H. Wu et al., "Weight-four parity checks in a spin-shuttling architecture," [Nature **655**, 1160–1166 (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10766-3).

## Key Results
- Built an effective five-qubit processor in isotopically purified $^{28}$Si/SiGe with a shuttling bus connecting four isolated interaction sites
- Demonstrated universal control, weight-four $X$- and $Z$-type parity checks, and genuine five-qubit GHZ entanglement
- Measured 97.7(1)% fidelity for a 1.2 μm round-trip shuttle operation in the five-qubit configuration, identifying transport and two-qubit operations as remaining bottlenecks
