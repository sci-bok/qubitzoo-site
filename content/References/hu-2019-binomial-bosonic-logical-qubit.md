This paper reports the first superconducting-cavity realization of a binomial bosonic logical qubit with repetitive quantum error correction and universal single-logical-qubit control.

## Key Results
- Demonstrates repetitive QEC on a binomial bosonic logical qubit in a cavity-transmon device
- Reports a universal single-logical-qubit gate set with 97% average process fidelity
- Shows a 2.8× lifetime improvement for the corrected logical qubit relative to the uncorrected logical qubit
- Reports roughly 2× Ramsey-coherence enhancement after repeated correction
