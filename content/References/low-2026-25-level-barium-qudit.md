Demonstrates coherent control, heralded state preparation, and single-shot readout of a 25-level qudit encoded across the $6S_{1/2}$ and $5D_{5/2}$ manifolds of a single $^{137}\mathrm{Ba}^+$ ion.

## Key Results
- Heralded average state-preparation-and-measurement fidelity of $99.51\pm0.05\%$ across 25 levels
- Coherence probes using superpositions of up to 24 states
- A three-virtual-qubit Bernstein--Vazirani algorithm and a four-virtual-qubit Toffoli gate in one ion
- A 16-level CCCNOT implementation with $99.5\pm0.2\%$ success probability
