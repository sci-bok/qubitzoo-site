Demonstrates mechanically dressed-state protection and ultrafast control of a SiV⁻ spin compatible with a phononic cavity. Author order was verified against the PDF title page.

## Key Results
- Mechanically driven Rabi frequency up to 800 MHz
- Optical initialization and readout in a noise-protected dressed basis
- Control remains compatible with resonant spin-phonon coupling
