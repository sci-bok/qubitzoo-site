Demonstrates simultaneous high-fidelity operation across the three core functions of a tunable-coupler transmon device rather than optimizing each function in isolation.

## Key Results
- 40-hour-averaged CZ gate fidelity: 99.93%.
- Simultaneous single-qubit gate fidelities: above 99.98%.
- Readout fidelities: above 99.94% with a 240 ns readout pulse.
- Total CZ duration: 33 ns in the reported benchmark.
- Introduces phased-averaged leakage error amplification (PALEA) for precise calibration of coherent CZ error and leakage.
