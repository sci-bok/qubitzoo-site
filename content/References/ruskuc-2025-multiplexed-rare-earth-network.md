Demonstrates a two-node nanophotonic network with multiple spectrally distinguishable $^{171}\mathrm{Yb}^{3+}$ ions. Frequency-erasing photon detection and real-time feedforward produced remote entanglement; multiplexing two ion pairs increased the entanglement rate, and three distinguishable ions were prepared in a multipartite W state.
