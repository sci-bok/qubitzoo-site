Studies Coulomb-blockaded hybrid InSb-Al islands and maps the transition between 2e-periodic even-parity, 2e-periodic odd-parity, and 1e-periodic ground states under magnetic field. The paper is important because it sharpened the experimental language around parity transitions and showed how zero-energy subgap states can mimic some Majorana-style signatures without yet proving a protected logical qubit.

## Key Results
- Magnetic-field-driven parity transitions in hybrid InSb/Al Coulomb islands
- Clear distinction between 2e-periodic even, 2e-periodic odd, and 1e-periodic regimes
- Experimental evidence for isolated zero-energy subgap states compatible with either trivial Andreev physics or Majorana phenomenology
- Useful benchmark for interpreting Coulomb-blockade signatures in Majorana-island experiments
