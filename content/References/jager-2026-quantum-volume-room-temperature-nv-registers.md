Room-temperature benchmarking of a four-qubit NV register with all-to-all connectivity.

## Key Results
- Measured single-qubit error-per-gate values from $1.0\times10^{-3}$ to $4.4\times10^{-3}$ across three nuclear-spin qubits
- Measured two-qubit error-per-gate values from $2.3\times10^{-2}$ to $4.7\times10^{-2}$ across all three qubit pairs
- Built a calibrated error model and estimated a room-temperature quantum volume of 8
