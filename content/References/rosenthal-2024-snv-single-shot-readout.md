Demonstrates single-shot readout of a SnV⁻ electronic spin and its compatibility with rapid microwave control. Author order was verified against the PDF title page.

## Key Results
- Raw single-shot readout fidelity: 87.4%
- Conditional fidelity using repeated readouts: 98.5%
- Weak-measurement protocol quantifies measurement-induced dephasing
