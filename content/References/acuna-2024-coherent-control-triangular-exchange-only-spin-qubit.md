This paper demonstrates coherent control of a triangular exchange-only spin qubit in Si/SiGe and is useful context for the later AEON experiment, but it is not itself an AEON demonstration.

## Key Results
- Demonstrates coherent control of a three-electron exchange-only qubit in a close-packed triangular quantum-dot geometry.
- Reports blind randomized-benchmarking single-qubit fidelity of 99.84%.
- Establishes the triangular Si/SiGe hardware platform later used for AEON-mode control.
- Clarifies that triangular geometry alone does not imply AEON operation.
