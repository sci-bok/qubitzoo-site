Uses tunable-coupler control to block-diagonalize the effective CZ Hamiltonian near spectator collisions, confining dynamics to an invariant two-dimensional subspace.

## Key Results
- Experimentally suppresses spectator leakage to the order of $10^{-4}$ across a wide near-resonant detuning range.
- Maintains suppression with up to three simultaneous spectator qubits.
- Demonstrates the protocol on a multiqubit superconducting processor with flux-tunable transmons and tunable couplers.
