Studies decoherence of an InAs crystal-phase-defined DQD charge qubit strongly coupled to a high-impedance microwave resonator.

## Key Results
- Extracted $g/2\pi = 320\,\text{MHz}$ and total decoherence $\Gamma/2\pi = 260\,\text{MHz}$ at the sweet spot.
- Found only a 10% change in decoherence despite a fivefold reduction in detuning-noise sensitivity.
- Identified electron-phonon relaxation and circuit loss as more plausible limits than detuning noise for this device.
