Demonstrates universal encoded exchange-only logic using two silicon three-spin qubits.

## Key Results
- Average two-qubit Clifford fidelity: 97.1 ± 0.2%.
- CNOT fidelity: 96.3 ± 0.7%.
- SWAP fidelity: 99.3 ± 0.5%.
- Demonstrates a leakage-containing entangling gate with 93.8 ± 0.7% fidelity.
