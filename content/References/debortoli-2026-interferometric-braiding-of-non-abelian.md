A proposal for impurity-assisted Ramsey interferometry of non-Abelian anyons and for transferring a conventional flying qubit into a topological memory.

## Key Results

- Uses mobile impurities with addressable internal states to measure matrix elements of the anyonic $R$ and $F$ matrices.
- Works through Ising and Fibonacci examples and proposes a conventional-to-topological qubit encoder.
- Remains a theory proposal; no experimental braid or encoder is reported.
