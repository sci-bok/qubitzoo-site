Proposes a three-electron hybrid qubit in a silicon double quantum dot. The two logical states share $S=1/2$ and $S_z=-1/2$ but differ in the singlet-versus-triplet character of the doubly occupied dot.

## Key Results
- Defines the logical singlet/triplet-coupled spin basis for three electrons in two dots.
- Shows how electrical control of singlet-triplet splittings and interdot tunnel coupling can implement fast gates.
- Establishes the double-dot architecture as a compact alternative to a three-dot exchange-only encoding.
