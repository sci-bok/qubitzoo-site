Demonstrates end-to-end error-corrected quantum phase estimation for molecular hydrogen using [[7,1,3]] color-code logical qubits plus real-time Steane QEC gadgets.

## Key Results
- Experimental energy estimate: E − E_FCI = 0.001(13) hartree
- Circuits used up to 1585 fixed and 7202 conditional physical two-qubit gates
