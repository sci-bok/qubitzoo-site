Ardavan et al. chemically assembled coupled Cr$_7$Ni molecular-spin dimers and used pulsed ESR to show that phase coherence survives while the inter-qubit interaction is tuned by molecular design.

## Key Results
- Demonstrated coherent molecular-spin dimers with chemically tunable interactions.
- For dimer 1A, reported $T_2\approx0.8\,\mu\text{s}$ and an interaction timescale $h/J\approx77\,\text{ns}$.
- Established a route toward two-qubit molecular-spin operations without reporting a randomized-benchmarking gate fidelity.
