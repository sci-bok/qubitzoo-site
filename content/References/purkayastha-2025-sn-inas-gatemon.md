Gatemon-like transmon using an InAs nanowire with a $\beta$-Sn superconducting shell, demonstrating that hybrid-junction coherence depends strongly on the materials stack.

## Key Results

- Gate tuning over a 3 GHz qubit-frequency range.
- Maximum $T_1=27\,\mu\text{s}$.
- Maximum echo $T_2=1.8\,\mu\text{s}$, reached at a different bias from maximum $T_1$.
