Comprehensive review of circuit QED, including the quantum-Rabi and Jaynes–Cummings models, dispersive readout, environmental coupling, quantum optics, and quantum-information applications.

## Key Results
- Reviews strong, dispersive, ultrastrong, and multimode light–matter coupling regimes.
- Derives the two-level and multilevel dispersive descriptions used for superconducting-qubit readout.
- Covers cavity-mediated gates, bosonic modes, microwave quantum optics, and hybrid systems.
