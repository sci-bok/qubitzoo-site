## Summary

Liquid-state NMR control benchmark extended to 12 nuclear spins. The experiment prepared a 12-coherence (pseudo-pure cat) state and decoded it to an 11-qubit-plus-one-qutrit labeled pseudo-pure state. It demonstrates sophisticated coherent control, not efficient scalable initialization or readout.
