Demonstrates leakage-resolved blind randomized benchmarking of an exchange-only qubit in isotopically enriched Si/SiGe.

## Key Results
- Average total error per unitary operation: 0.35%.
- Leakage contribution: 0.17%, attributed primarily to substrate nuclear spins.
- Uses only voltage-controlled exchange for qubit manipulation.
