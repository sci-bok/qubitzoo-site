Field-gradient-driven $S$–$T_0$ operation in an isotopically enriched silicon double quantum dot with an on-chip micromagnet.

## Key Results
- Approximately 100 MHz singlet-triplet oscillations.
- Oscillation quality factor above 580.
- $T_2^*<1\,\mu$s in the reported weak-field ergodic regime.
