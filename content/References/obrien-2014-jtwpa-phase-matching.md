Introduces resonant phase matching for a Josephson-junction traveling-wave parametric amplifier, enabling modeled 20 dB gain over 3 GHz with a saturation power of −98 dBm.

## Key Results
- Resonant inclusions engineer the dispersion needed for phase matching
- 20 dB target gain over 3 GHz instantaneous bandwidth
- −98 dBm saturation power in the proposed design
