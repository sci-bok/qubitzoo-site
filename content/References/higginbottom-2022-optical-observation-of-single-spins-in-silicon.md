Landmark experimental paper reporting optical observation of individual T-centre spins in silicon. It moves the platform from ensemble or interface demonstrations into the single-spin regime needed for network-node style quantum hardware.

## Key Results
- Optical observation of individual T-centre spins in silicon
- Single-spin readout in cavity-coupled silicon nanophotonic devices
- Strong evidence that T centres can function as silicon-native spin-photon nodes rather than only as ensemble emitters
