Independent 2015 demonstration of microwave circuits built from InAs/NbTiN nanowire Josephson elements. Capacitively shunted single elements behaved as voltage-tunable transmon-like qubits; a two-element loop exposed the non-sinusoidal current-phase relation near half flux.

## Key Results

- Electrostatic tuning of a semiconductor weak-link Josephson energy.
- Transmon-like operation for a single shunted nanowire element.
- Flux-qubit-like double-well behavior in a two-element loop near half flux; this was not a two-gatemon entangling-gate result.
